About Me

Your Photo

Hello! I'm Pushkar Dube.

I'm a B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering student at VJTI, Mumbai, and pursuing a parallel B.S in Data Science from IIT Madras. I specialize in embedded systems, robotics, PCB design, and full-stack web development.

I'm an active contributor at the Society of Robotics and Automation (SRA) at VJTI, where I work on robotics projects and embedded systems workshops. I love designing hardware systems end-to-end — from PCB schematics in KiCad to firmware on ESP32 to mechanical CAD in Onshape.

Audio Looper

A compact, battery-powered audio looper using two ISD1820 voice recording ICs controlled by an ESP32. Records Track 1, overdubs Track 2 while playing back Track 1, and outputs a combined loop via a passive resistor-based audio mixer to a speaker. Built with FSM sequencing, edge/level triggering, and precise timing coordination.
