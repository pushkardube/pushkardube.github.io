About Me

The operator behind the signals

Hello! I'm Pushkar Dube.

I'm an embedded & wireless-security engineer — a B.Tech Electronics & Telecommunication student at VJTI Mumbai, with a parallel B.S. in Data Science from IIT Madras. I build radio systems from the physical layer up: Ultra-Wideband ranging stacks, secure-ranging attacks, RF PCBs, and the firmware underneath.

I like owning a system end-to-end — schematic and layout in KiCad, firmware on ESP32 and Zephyr, mechanical CAD in Onshape — and then breaking it to understand how it really behaves. Lately that's meant physical-layer attacks on commercial secure-ranging devices and machine-learning approaches to wireless localization.

I'm Sponsorship Head at the Society of Robotics & Automation (SRA) at VJTI, where I've run embedded-firmware and PCB workshops for 400+ first-year students — from line-following Wall-E bots and image-processing to ROS2 manipulators.

Research & Security

UWB Ranging Security Research COSIC

imec-COSIC, KU Leuven, Belgium · Jun 2026 – Present

Physical-layer security research on Ultra-Wideband ranging. Built SS/DS-TWR and STS-secured (IEEE 802.15.4z) ranging firmware from scratch on the Qorvo DWM3001CDK in Zephyr — antenna-delay calibration, delayed-TX scheduling, CIA timestamp gating. Reproduced and bench-verified real-hardware attacks — Cicada pulse-train, Ghost Peak distance reduction (~5 m against an STS-secured link), and UWBAD selective/flood jamming — against Qorvo CLI firmware and the Apple NI iPhone link. Reverse-engineered live FiRa and Apple NI traffic from passive captures and built a FAST attack-success pipeline with Clopper–Pearson intervals.

UWB DW3000 Zephyr RTOS 802.15.4z RF Security

Urey-ML — RL Attack-Parameter Optimizer Ongoing

A reproduction of Urey-ML (Liu et al., IEEE TIFS 2026): a deep-reinforcement-learning agent (DQN) that learns to tune a UWB distance-reduction attack's transmit power and delay so the forged range mimics natural motion and stays under the ranging-awareness kill-switch. Converges in simulation to a stealthy, high-yield policy; real-DW3000/iPhone hardware hooks are in progress.

Reinforcement Learning DQN PyTorch UWB DW3000

WiFi CSI Localization Ongoing

Indoor localization and sensing from WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) on ESP32. Capturing per-subcarrier amplitude and phase with the ESP-CSI framework and processing it to infer position and motion — non-contact, infrastructure-free sensing that rides on ordinary WiFi. Data pipeline and estimation are under active development.
